Breakfast Sausage

Prep Time Cook Time Total Time
25 mins 10 mins 35 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 cup TVP
  • 1 cup warm vegetable broth
  • ½ tsp fennel seeds
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp ground sage
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp kitchen banquet
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • ½ tsp liquid smoke
  • 2 tbsp ground flaxseeds
  • 15 g all-purpose flour
  • ¾ cup panko or quick oats

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, add TVP and pour warm vegetable broth over it. Let the TVP rehydrate for about 15 minutes or until it has soaked up the broth.
  2. Now add all other ingredients to the bowl and stir to combine. If the mixture seems to wet to hold together, add additional oats. Form patties with your hands.
  3. Heat a few tablespoons of oil in a cast iron pan, then pan fry the sausage patties for 3-4 minutes on each side until browned. Serve immediately or store in the refrigerator for up to 4 days. They can also be frozen. Reheat in an air fryer, microwave or oven until warm.
